# MatchOS demo identity

## Overview
Use the actual MatchOS identity captured from its deployed Azure application. The film feels like a precise football broadcast studio: forest-green surfaces, warm gold wayfinding, off-white headlines and restrained team colors. Real interface recordings and actual model outputs carry the proof. Preserve their original pixels rather than grading them.

## Colors
- Ground `#07110d`: full film canvas.
- Secondary surface `#0c1914`: panels and diagram stages.
- Card `#101c17`: raised content.
- Ink `#f3f6f2`: primary type.
- Muted `#9aafa4`: supporting text.
- Gold `#e6c36a`: brand rules, key moments and active paths.
- Liverpool `#e94c55`: fan and goal accents.
- Manchester City `#8cc8eb`: opposition and neutral analysis cues.
- Grass `#146845`, `#18784e`: pitch motif and depth.
- Broadcaster violet `#bc9be8`: drawn from the microphone icon in the live interface.

## Typography
- Outfit (300–800), the captured product font: headlines and explanatory prose. This is intentional brand matching despite its presence on the generic-font avoidance list.
- IBM Plex Mono (400–500): timecodes, pipeline labels, provider names, status badges.
- Film headlines 64–100px, body 28–36px, data labels at least 20px. Actual product captures may contain smaller source text; crop and enlarge focal areas rather than asking the audience to read the entire UI.

## Elevation
Use fine warm-white borders, dark-green surface steps and moderate 20–28px radii. Depth comes from framed screen footage and separated editorial cards, not neon glows. Keep footage fully opaque and brand colors unchanged. A subtle pitch-line motif can move behind the content.

## Components
- MO roundel and MatchOS wordmark, faithfully derived from the product's text-based mark.
- Broadcast timecode and three checkpoint chips: press 64:00, goal 65:04, dismissal 68:01.
- Three persona columns: microphone / fan flag / analysis bars, using the product SVG shapes.
- Evidence pipeline: state → verified candidates → independent editorial selection → final copy.
- Captured model-output quotes, always exact excerpts from `demo-results.json`.
- Azure deployment topology, with implemented services clearly separated from the future roadmap.

## Do's and Don'ts
### Do
- Lead with real footage and let the audience read each persona angle.
- Reveal connected architecture in causal order.
- Use 0.45-second scene-level crossfades; every scene has entrances and a calm held composition.
- Use finite ambient motion on a separate wrapper from entrance motion.
- Label simulated match footage, actual generated output, grading workflow and future concepts honestly.
### Don't
- Do not invent model responses, grading scores, benchmark claims or production scale.
- Do not change the colors of the real screen capture.
- Do not show credentials, portal keys or terminal environment files.
- Do not use slides full of small prose or three identical decorative cards without meaning.
- No exit animation before a scene transition; no infinite timelines.
