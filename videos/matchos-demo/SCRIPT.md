# MatchOS — One match. Every perspective.

Two-minute landscape judge demo. Calm, clear product narration; free local Kokoro Nova voice. All product outputs come from the authenticated Azure deployment. Architecture and future shots are explanatory motion graphics. The source match is simulated.

## 01 · Hook · 00:00–00:08
One match. Three viewers. Why should they all hear the same story? This is Match O S.

## 02 · The press · 00:08–00:32
Here's the same moment through three perspectives. The broadcaster gets a concise, neutral cue: Liverpool are getting through City's press. The Liverpool fan sees what that means for their team. The analyst gets the before-and-after numbers. Each viewer makes an independent editorial choice from shared evidence, including the choice to hold a story.

## 03 · Goal · 00:32–00:42
Then Liverpool score. The broadcaster leads with the score change. The fan gets Liverpool's story. The analyst retains the match context.

## 04 · Red card · 00:42–00:52
City's red card changes the angle again. A neutral dismissal update, Liverpool's player advantage, and the numbers behind the new match situation.

## 05 · Agent framework · 00:52–01:14
Microsoft Agent Framework orchestrates the pipeline. Deterministic match state produces evidence-backed candidates. Each persona independently asks Microsoft's Foundry Decision One which story matters, or whether to wait. G P T six Luna turns selected facts into tailored copy. Production rules limit repeats, while asynchronous model calls let the pitch keep flowing.

## 06 · Traces and grading · 01:14–01:32
Execution telemetry exposes deterministic inputs, model calls, outputs and publication decisions. Compact traces flow to Application Insights and Foundry. Saved runs can go to four separate Foundry graders: editorial selection, persona quality, semantic grounding and restraint. Grading stays outside live playback.

## 07 · Deployed on Azure · 01:32–01:46
The app is deployed on Azure Container Apps, with a private container registry, managed identity, Key Vault secrets and password-protected access. Azure Speech can read aired stories aloud. The deployment includes health probes and rollback.

## 08 · Future · 01:46–02:00
Next: licensed live feeds, deeper player perspectives, and shared state for reconnecting viewers and horizontal scale. One match. Every perspective. A useful story, grounded in evidence.
