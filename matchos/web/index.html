    How well does MatchOS handle the match?

    We replay known synthetic situations and check whether MatchOS shows the right kind of update, uses supported facts, and stays quiet when it should.

    Choose a previously completed batch of tests by date and model setup. Changing this selection loads saved results; it does not test the match currently playing or call a model.

    1. A known match situation

    A goal, a red card, quiet play or an update that has already aired. Each situation has an expected behavior.

    2. An audience perspective

    “Viewer” means an AI audience profile, not a person watching the app: a neutral Broadcaster, a Liverpool supporter or a neutral Analyst.

    3. One test result per perspective

    The same situation is checked separately for each profile. Eight situations × three perspectives produce 24 results, not 24 matches.
